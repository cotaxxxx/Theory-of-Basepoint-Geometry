# Basepoint Geometry: Particular Studies II

**基点幾何論・各論Ⅱ**

## Working scope

Particular Studies II treats changes in stationary-basepoint structure under deformation of the geometric object:

- stationary-orbit bifurcation;
- symmetry breaking;
- unfolding of degenerate stationary sets;
- transitions between smooth and nonsmooth families;
- polyhedral, frustum, and related deformation models.

## Role in the theory

These studies test how the structures identified in Particular Studies I persist, split, enter through a boundary, or disappear as geometry and symmetry change. Their common mechanisms are candidates for the bifurcation and stability portions of the General Theory.

## Boundary with Basepoint Dynamics

This part studies changes in stationary structures as geometric parameters vary. It does not yet study time evolution, attracting basins, or stable and unstable manifolds. Those belong to the later program of Basepoint Dynamics.

## Evidence boundary

Object-specific implementations and certificates remain in their originating repositories. This paper workspace records only statements whose provenance and claim scope can be traced explicitly.
