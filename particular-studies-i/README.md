# Basepoint Geometry: Particular Studies I

**基点幾何論・各論Ⅰ**

## Working scope

Particular Studies I treats the stationary-basepoint geometry of smooth quadratic families:

- the sphere;
- prolate spheroids;
- oblate spheroids;
- triaxial ellipsoids.

The central questions are the location, number, symmetry type, and Morse character of stationary base points, together with the parameter values at which their structure changes.

## Role in the theory

These concrete families provide the first controlled setting in which the common language and theorem candidates of Basepoint Geometry can be tested. Statements that are independent of the chosen quadratic body are candidates for transfer to the General Theory.

## Evidence boundary

Each numerical value must retain the derivation class and claim scope assigned by its originating repository. High-precision agreement is not a certified enclosure. Experimental repositories remain the source of implementation code, diagnostics, and certificates.
