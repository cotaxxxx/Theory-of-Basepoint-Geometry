# Basepoint Geometry: General Theory

**基点幾何論・総論**

## Purpose

The General Theory will identify and prove the structures that do not depend on a single geometric family. It is to be extracted from the completed particular studies rather than imposed on them in advance.

## Provisional scope

- the basic data and framework ((P,\Lambda,E));
- stationary base points and stationary orbits;
- symmetry and equivariance;
- Morse and Morse–Bott structures;
- local reduction and bifurcation;
- stability under nondegeneracy assumptions;
- evidence, reconstruction, and claim scope.

## Drafting rule

While Particular Studies I–II are being developed, each reusable statement should be recorded first as a general-theory seed in the project-level repository. A seed becomes part of this paper only after its hypotheses and proof scope are clear.

## Timing

The main General Theory paper is planned after the first four particular papers. An earlier feasibility check may be made for the stability theorem, because the attainable hypotheses will determine the eventual scope of the paper.
